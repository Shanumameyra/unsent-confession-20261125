One micro-confession in exactly two parts: a `To:` line naming who it was
meant for, and the unsent message itself — one line, written exactly as it
was typed, with no explanation after it.

## The confession

> To: my driving instructor
>
> i passed my test because you pretended not to see me stall at the
> roundabout, and i never told anyone, not even my wife, that my licence
> is built on your kindness.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the confession to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that is set). Deterministic: the same
bytes on every run. Total length 190 bytes, within the 1–260 character gate;
the message line is 163 characters, within the 200-character limit.
